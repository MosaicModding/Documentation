The **Bouldering Zombie** is a new Zombie variant that is home to the deepslate layers of the world and can climb. <br/>
It is taller than a regular Zombie and deals more damage.<br/>
However, the **Bouldering Zombie** is very sensitive to _fire_, meaning that it will deal `1.5` times as much damage.
