The Lanternfish is a new Mob that tries to avoid light and glows. When the Lanternfish is hurt it will stop glowing for a few seconds. <br/>
On death the Lanternfish drops `Raw Lanternfish` and has a `0.05` chance of dropping Bonemeal.
