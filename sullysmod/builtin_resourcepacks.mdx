Builtin Resource Packs are included in the mod and can be activated when the mod is installed.

## Mob Retextures
This Pack updates a few outdated looking mobs to fit better with Sully's Mod.
The mobs currently included are:
- Zombie
- Drowned
- Husk
- Cave Spider
- Skeleton Horse

## Programmer Art Pack
This pack adds programmer art textures for Sully's Mod. <br/>
This pack is currently work in progress and is not yet included in the mod.
