Each **Jungle Spider** has two venoms, one random _beneficial_ effect and one random _harmful_ effect. <br/>
When a **Jungle Spider** attacks the player, it will give the player these effects.
The two colours on the **Jungle Spider's** back can help indicate the effects of the venom.

The effect duration depends on the game's difficulty. <br/>

| Difficulty | Duration |
|------------|----------|
| Easy       | 5 sec    |
| Normal     | 10 sec   |
| Hard       | 15 sec   |

## Glass Vial
<ShowcaseBlock image="img/venom_vials.gif">
    You can craft a **Glass Vial**, which you can use to collect the **Jungle Spider's** venom. <br/>
    When drinking, both effects will be applied.
</ShowcaseBlock>
