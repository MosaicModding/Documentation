The **Piranha** is a fish that spawns in groups in jungle biomes and the mangrove swamp. <br/>
It is only aggressive to small entities, entities that do not have full health, entities that attacked it and boats.
Besides boats, it will only attack organic mobs! <br/>
If the **Piranha** kills an entity that drops meat, it will consume the meat and heal slightly. <br/>

If the **Piranha** kills a zombie or a zombie horse, they will turn into a skeleton or a skeleton horse. <br/>
The **Piranha** drops `Raw Piranha` and has a `0.27` chance of dropping a `Piranha Tooth`.
