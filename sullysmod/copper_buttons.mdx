## Pulse Lengths
The further Copper Buttons are oxidized, the longer their redstone pulse lasts. <br/>

| Oxidization State | Pulse Length |
|-------------------|--------------|
| Unaffected        | 10 ticks     |
| Exposed           | 20 ticks     |
| Weathered         | 30 ticks     |
| Oxidized          | 40 ticks     |
